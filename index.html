          AM Quarterly Bonus — How it works Managed book size × quarterly net revenue retention
          An Account Manager's quarterly bonus rewards two things at once: the size of the book you manage and how much you grow it. A larger managed book sets a higher baseline bonus; stronger expansion (Quarterly NRR) multiplies it. Expansion comes from revenue-share and usage growth, added seats and execs, added scope, and upsells & cross-sells — with contraction and churn netted out. New customers assigned mid-quarter grow your book size but don't count toward the existing-cohort NRR.
          50 / 50
          Half your bonus is your own performance · half is the team's
          Every AM's quarterly bonus is 50% individual + 50% team. Your half runs on your Quarterly NRR; the team half runs on the whole team's combined NRR — both measured on the same scale below and paid on your book's baseline. Pick “Team (whole book)” in the calculator to see where the team stands.
          STEP 1
          Book Band
          Your total managed book falls into a band ($50k–$75k … $1M+). The band sets your baseline bonus — the amount earned at 110% NRR.
          STEP 2
          Quarterly NRR
          Ending ÷ beginning MRR for your existing-customer cohort — how much you grew the book you started the quarter with. Net expansion = expansion − contraction − churn; new logos are excluded.
          STEP 3
          Bonus = Baseline × Multiplier
          How much you grew sets a multiplier — 1.0× at 110% NRR up to 6.0× at 200%+. Bonus = band baseline × multiplier, capped at 6% of the expansion you generated (so it never outpaces AE commission).
          NOTE
          Churn Disqualifier
          2 churned customers in one month disqualifies the entire quarterly bonus — regardless of expansion.
